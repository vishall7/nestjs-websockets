# Project Workflow

This document tracks the current product workflow for the B2B software-services marketplace. It is a living document and should be updated as requirements are added, changed, or removed.

## Current Workflow

1. Customer posts a requirement.
2. System matches suitable vendors.
3. Vendor receives a notification.
4. Vendor pays wallet credits to unlock the customer's contact details.
5. Customer and vendor continue communication through email or phone.
6. Platform tracks the lead status:
   - `matched`
   - `unlocked`
   - `contacted`
   - `won`
   - `closed`

## Product Scope Notes

- Vendors can register as freelancers or agencies.
- Full in-platform chat is not part of the initial workflow.
- The workflow may change as the project is discussed and implemented.
